The problem

Growth creates complexity.

Every stage of growth adds another tool, another vendor and another opinion. Eventually the systems that got you here start slowing you down — and nobody quite owns the whole picture.

⧉

Too many tools

Overlapping software nobody fully uses, quietly billing every month.

◑

Too many opinions

Decisions stall because there's no shared view of what good looks like.

◈

Too many vendors

Work is split across suppliers who each solve only their own slice.

◎

Not enough ownership

Plenty of advice, far less accountability for the outcome.
